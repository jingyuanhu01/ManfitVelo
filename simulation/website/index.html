Simulation Vector Fields

Small internal reference page for synthesizing the current simulation catalog. Python remains the source of truth for dataset generation.

Generation Contract

flat manifold, rotation field

Tangential field on an annulus: [-y, x].

flat manifold, spiral field

Linear outward rotation: [x - y, x + y].

flat manifold, saddle field

Rotated hyperbolic flow with eigenvalues -1, +1.

flat manifold, quadratic source sink field

Complex-square-style field: [x^2 - y^2 - 4, 2xy].

flat manifold, radial source field

Outward radial field: [x, y].

flat manifold, radial sink field

Inward radial field: [-x, -y].

flat manifold, horizontal shear field

Horizontal shear by vertical position: [y, 0].

flat manifold, sinusoidal field

Smooth periodic field: [sin(pi y), cos(pi x)].

s curve manifold, velocity flow field

3D S-curve with tangent flow along the rolled coordinate.

swiss roll manifold, velocity flow field

3D Swiss roll with tangent flow along the spiral coordinate.

half sphere manifold, velocity flow field

Upper hemisphere with tangent rotation around the vertical axis.

half sphere manifold, rotation field

Tangent rotation around the vertical axis.

half sphere manifold, spiral field

Outward rotation projected onto the sphere tangent plane.

half sphere manifold, saddle field

Hyperbolic tangent field projected onto the upper hemisphere.

half sphere manifold, radial source field

Meridian-like outward source flow on the curved surface.

half sphere manifold, radial sink field

Meridian-like inward sink flow on the curved surface.

half sphere manifold, quadratic source sink field

Quadratic source/sink field projected onto the half-sphere.
